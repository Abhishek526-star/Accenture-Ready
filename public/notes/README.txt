PDF Notes Directory:
Place your PDF files inside the respective subfolders:
- public/notes/operating-system/
- public/notes/computer-network/
- public/notes/cloud/
- public/notes/dbms/
- public/notes/oops/

Once placed, you can also update or add new entries in `src/data/notesConfig.js`.
You can also use the in-app "Upload / Preview Local PDF" button directly on the /notes page to test or preview any PDF instantly!
